Torrent expands Local Police recruitment to 27 positions

The City Council has expanded the current recruitment process for Local Police agents to include 27 positions, adding 15 new vacancies from the 2026 Public Employment Offer. This move aims to strengthen the Neighborhood Police unit, a commitment made by Mayor Amparo Folgado in 2023. The 27 positions are split into 18 open-competition slots and 9 internal mobility slots. Applications remain open until November 9, allowing residents to apply for these new roles in municipal security.

Restoration begins on 16th-century Cabreve document

The City Council has initiated the restoration of the Cabreve of the Torrent and Picanya commandery, a document dated between 1592 and 1599. This historical record, held in the Municipal Historical Archive, offers insights into the territory's organization and daily life over four centuries ago. The specialized work will be carried out by the Valencian Institute for Conservation, Restoration and Research (IVCR+i) over approximately one year. This project aims to preserve one of the oldest documents in the city's archive for future generations.

Mayor receives 72 students from CEIP Sant Pasqual

Mayor Amparo Folgado received 72 fourth-grade students from CEIP Sant Pasqual at the City Hall this week. The visit was the final activity of the students' educational project about Torrent, allowing them to see how municipal decisions are made firsthand. The students were welcomed in the Plenary Hall, where the mayor answered their questions about the city and the functioning of the council. This initiative aims to foster civic engagement and understanding of local governance among young residents.

Torrent hosts blood donation sessions next week

The Valencian Community Blood Transfusion Center will visit Torrent next week to facilitate blood donations. The municipality will host three donation days at different locations in the city. This initiative is particularly important following the severe railway accident in Urda (Toledo), which has increased the demand for blood. The city council encourages residents to participate in these sessions to support the victims and their families. The sessions aim to ensure an adequate supply of blood for medical needs.

Police have detained the suspected founder and promoter of the 'Blocus' movement in Spain in Picanya (Valencia).
