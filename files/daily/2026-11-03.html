New ITV station operational in Torrent

Torrent now has a vehicle inspection station (ITV) that began operating on Monday, October 26, 2026. The new facility allows drivers from Torrent and other l'Horta Sud municipalities to perform mandatory vehicle checks locally. This eliminates the need to travel to other towns for inspections. The station offers specific hours and an appointment system for residents to manage their vehicle maintenance.
