New provisional ITV station opens at Mas del Jutge

Torrent has inaugurated a new provisional vehicle inspection station (ITV) located at the Mas del Jutge industrial park. The facility, situated on Carrer dels Rajolers, features two inspection lines and eight inspectors. It is designed to handle approximately 100 to 120 inspections per day, significantly reducing wait times for residents of Torrent and the Horta Sud region. The opening was attended by Mayor Amparo Folgado and the Director General of Industry, Julio Delgado. This provisional setup will operate for the next two years until a permanent station is built.

€1.3 million allocated for school climate control improvements

The City Council has convened an extraordinary Municipal School Council to announce a plan to improve thermal comfort in public schools. The project involves an investment of 1.3 million euros to upgrade heating and cooling systems, aiming to combat extreme temperatures for students and staff. A temporary commission will be created to allow the educational community to monitor the progress of the works and propose further improvements. The meeting serves to inform parents and teachers about the specific actions planned for each center.

Female football growth in Torrent

Female football in Torrent continues to grow, with 15 teams participating in the league this season. The number of licensed players has exceeded 230, including those playing in mixed teams. This expansion is led by clubs such as Torrent CF and CFF Torrent L’Horta Sud. The increase in participation reflects a broader trend in the region, with more girls joining the sport at various levels. The league's start marks another successful season for local women's football.

Fire on balcony in Benetússer extinguished

Firefighters from the Valencia Fire Brigade responded to a fire on the balcony of a residence in Benetússer around 9:00 AM. Two units from Torrent and Silla, along with a sergeant from Torrent, were mobilized to the scene after receiving an alert. The fire was successfully extinguished, preventing further damage to the property. The incident highlights the ongoing readiness of local emergency services to respond to residential fires in the area.
