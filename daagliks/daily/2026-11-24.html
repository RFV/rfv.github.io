Council approves €826,578 for four EDIL-DANA projects

The Torrent City Council unanimously approved the municipal contribution of €826,578 to four projects under the EDIL-DANA plan. This funding, part of a €12 million European investment program, will be used for flood prevention measures, water supply improvements, public housing, and emergency response technology. The decision allows the city to advance the administrative process for these critical infrastructure works aimed at strengthening resilience against future floods.

€3 million approved for CEIP San Juan Bautista renovation

The Local Government Board approved the basic and execution project for the comprehensive renovation of the CEIP San Juan Bautista school. With a budget exceeding €3 million, the works will include improvements in accessibility, climate control, energy efficiency, courtyards, installations, and educational spaces. This project is part of the municipal plan to modernize public infrastructure and improve the learning environment for students in the municipality.

New campaign to prevent sexual aggression in nightlife venues

The City Council, through the Casa de la Dona, launched a campaign on Monday, November 23, to prevent sexual aggression in nightlife establishments. The initiative, part of the 25N programming, involves the hospitality sector by placing informational stickers in women's restrooms. These stickers provide resources for help and recommendations for action in case of potential aggression, aiming to ensure no woman is alone facing danger in these venues.

Man arrested for throwing 13kg cobblestone at another's head

National Police agents arrested a 21-year-old man in Torrent as the alleged perpetrator of attempted homicide. The suspect allegedly threw a 13kg cobblestone at another man's head on the terrace of a local establishment following a dispute. The incident has sparked concern in the local community regarding public safety.

PP highlights 85 protected homes built in Torrent

Susana Camarero, the PP candidate for the Senate by Valencia, visited Torrent accompanied by local PP president Amparo Folgado to highlight the construction of 85 protected homes. The visit emphasized the allocation of 57 homes in Benisaet and 28 industrialized construction units, representing an investment of over €16 million. The PP contrasts this with the previous eight years of socialist government, claiming no protected housing was built during that period.

Torrent exhibits historical documents from Jaume I's era

The city is displaying historical documents linked to King Jaume I and the origins of Torrent, including the 1248 Carta de Poblament. The exhibition, inaugurated at the Casa de la Cultura, is part of the 'Any Jaume I' programming marking the 750th anniversary of the monarch's death. Visitors can see these foundational documents until November 26, offering insight into the city's medieval history and territorial development.
