Torrent approves grants for anti-flood barriers

The Town Hall has approved a new line of grants to subsidize the purchase of anti-flood barriers for homes, shops, and community buildings. The initial budget is €50,000, covering up to 70% of the cost with a maximum of €500 per property. Priority will be given to properties affected by the floods of 16 September 2026. The call is expandable and aims to protect residents from future flooding events.

€595,000 for public school maintenance

Torrent has increased the budget for maintaining public schools to €595,000, including an additional €150,000. The funds will be used for repairs to roofs, courtyards, access points, and children's areas, as well as improvements to electrical installations and climate control systems. The works will cover six specific school centers and general maintenance across all public schools in the municipality. This investment aims to improve the safety and comfort of educational facilities.

€680,000 for public lighting improvements

The Town Hall has approved a new investment of €383,000 to improve public lighting, bringing the total project value to nearly €680,000. Works include replacing 270 deteriorated poles in Santa Apolonia and installing 123 new LED points on Avenida al Vedat. The project also covers industrial zones, scattered settlements, and a bike lane. These improvements aim to enhance safety and visibility across the municipality.

General strike loses union support

The general strike called for 11 November is losing support from several unions, including CSIF, USO, and Fetico, as well as SPPLB in Torrent. These organizations have distanced themselves from the call, citing its political nature during the pre-campaign period. Finance Minister María Jesús Montero acknowledged that the strike is directed against the opposition rather than the government. Feijóo and Tellado have denounced it as an electoral operation by unions.
