The parish of Nuestra Señora de Monte-Sión hosted the solemn Canonical Coronation of the image of Nuestra Señora de los Dolores on Sunday. The ceremony was presided over by the Archbishop of Valencia, Enrique Benavent, and attended by local authorities, representatives of brotherhoods, and numerous devotees.
