Torrent has inaugurated a new provisional vehicle inspection station (ITV) located at the Mas del Jutge industrial park. The facility, situated on Carrer dels Rajolers, features two inspection lines and eight inspectors. It is designed to handle approximately 100 to 120 inspections per day, significantly reducing wait times for residents of Torrent and the Horta Sud region.
