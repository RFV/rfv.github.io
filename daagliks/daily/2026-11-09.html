€200,000 allocated for accessibility and facade rehabilitation grants

The City Council has approved a new call for grants totaling 200,000 euros to improve accessibility and rehabilitate facades of residential buildings in Torrent. Subsidies will cover up to 50% of construction costs, with individual grants reaching up to 4,000 euros, or 8,000 euros for the first installation of elevators. The call covers works carried out since 16 November 2024 and includes both multi-family buildings and single-family homes. The regulatory bases and call have been elevated to the Local Government Board for approval.

CEIP Sant Pasqual to receive €65,000 for sports court and accessibility upgrades

The Local Government Board approved the tender for improvements to the sports courts and accessibility of CEIP Sant Pasqual, with a budget of approximately 65,000 euros. The works are part of the Plan Edificant and are expected to take two months to complete. The project will enhance the educational center's sports facilities and improve access to the schoolyards. This investment aims to modernize the infrastructure and ensure better accessibility for students and staff.

Conservatorio de Música named after Amparo Fandos Miquel

The City Council of Torrent approved a proposal from the PSOE to name the Conservatorio de Música after Amparo Fandos Miquel. The decision was made unanimously during the plenary session. This recognition honors the legacy of Amparo Fandos Miquel, a significant figure in the local community. The naming reflects the city's commitment to preserving and celebrating the contributions of its residents to the cultural and musical life of Torrent.

Firmus Homes launches second phase of Vergel Residencial

Firmus Homes has started the second phase of its Vergel Residencial development in Torrent, which includes 92 new homes. The project is located 15 minutes from Valencia, offering residents convenient access to the capital. This phase continues the company's commitment to expanding residential options in the area. The development aims to provide modern housing solutions for families and individuals seeking a balance between urban convenience and suburban living.
