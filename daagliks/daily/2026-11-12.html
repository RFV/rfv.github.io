Torrent excluded from Casa 47 affordable housing despite offering land

Torrent is not among the four municipalities selected by Casa 47 for the first phase of affordable housing construction on DANA-affected municipal land. The city offered a plot on Juan XXIII street but was not chosen. The four selected towns—Alaquàs, Cheste, and others—have PSOE mayors. Torrent is demanding an explanation for the valuation received for its plot.

Torrent buys nine DANA-affected plots to prevent new construction

The city council has approved the acquisition of nine plots devastated by the DANA to incorporate them into municipal property. The goal is to convert these areas into green spaces and prevent new construction in high-risk zones. This move follows the exclusion of Torrent from the Casa 47 housing program despite its offer of land for affordable homes.
