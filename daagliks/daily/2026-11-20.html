Torrent celebrates World Children's Day with a school march and a fair

The City Council of Torrent marked the International Day of the Rights of the Child on 20 November with a series of activities organized by the Youth and Childhood department. Special education center La Unión receives €674,680 for maintenance

The Consell has approved a collaboration agreement between the Education, Culture and Universities Department and the Intermunicipal Community of L'Horta Sud to fund the maintenance of the La Unión Special Education Center in Torrent. The center will receive a subsidy of €674,680. This financial contribution is intended to cover the operational costs of the facility, ensuring the continued provision of specialized educational services in the region.

PSPV proposes covering stands and repairing football fields

The PSPV of Torrent has presented a motion to the municipal plenary requesting the installation of covers to provide shade on the stands of the football fields at Parc Central and San Gregorio B. National Police dismantle cocaine sales point in Torrent

National Police agents dismantled a cocaine sales point operating in a townhouse in Torrent and detained its two residents, a man and a woman, as suspected authors of a drug trafficking crime. Man sentenced for sexually assaulting a minor in Torrent

The Audiencia of Valencia sentenced a man from Torrent to six years and nine months in prison for sexually assaulting a minor.
