### [Restoration begins on 16th-century Cabreve document](https://infotorrent.es/alcaldia/torrent-inicia-la-restauracion-de-un-cabreve-de-finales-del-siglo-xvi-uno-de-los-documentos-mas-antiguos-de-su-archivo-historico)
The City Council has initiated the restoration of the Cabreve of the Torrent and Picanya commandery, a document dated between 1592 and 1599. This historical record, held in the Municipal Historical Archive, offers insights into the territory's organization and daily life over four centuries ago. The specialized work will be carried out by the Valencian Institute for Conservation, Restoration and Research (IVCR+i) over approximately one year. This project aims to preserve one of the oldest documents in the city's archive for future generations.
