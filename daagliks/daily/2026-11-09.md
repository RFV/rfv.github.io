### [€200,000 allocated for accessibility and facade rehabilitation grants](https://infotorrent.es/urbanismo/torrent-destina-200-000-euros-a-ayudas-para-mejorar-la-accesibilidad-y-rehabilitar-fachadas-de-edificios-residenciales)
The City Council has approved a new call for grants totaling 200,000 euros to improve accessibility and rehabilitate facades of residential buildings in Torrent. Subsidies will cover up to 50% of construction costs, with individual grants reaching up to 4,000 euros, or 8,000 euros for the first installation of elevators. The call covers works carried out since 16 November 2024 and includes both multi-family buildings and single-family homes. The regulatory bases and call have been elevated to the Local Government Board for approval.
