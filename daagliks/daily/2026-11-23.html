Torrent celebrates special 'Rutes del Temps' edition dedicated to Any Jaume I

The Torrent City Council, through the Culture Department, will hold a new edition of 'Rutes del Temps per Torrent' on Saturday 28 November. This special route, titled 'Torrent Medieval, especial Any Jaume I', invites residents and visitors to discover the city's history and heritage. The event will take place at 11:00 and features a theatrical performance by Cucurucú Teatre, with historical commentary provided by local chronicler José Royo. It is a free activity aimed at commemorating the medieval past of the municipality.

Torrent marks historic day with Canonical Coronation of Our Lady of Sorrows

The parish of Nuestra Señora de Monte-Sión hosted the solemn Canonical Coronation of the image of Nuestra Señora de los Dolores on Sunday. The ceremony was presided over by the Archbishop of Valencia, Enrique Benavent, and attended by local authorities, representatives of brotherhoods, and numerous devotees. This event marks a historic moment for the city, occurring 75 years after the first canonical coronation was held in Torrent. The image belongs to the Muy Ilustre Hermandad de Nuestra Señora de los Dolores.

Local shops join 25N campaign against violence against women

The Torrent City Council, through the Casa de la Dona, has launched the campaign 'Red de comercios de Torrent 100% por la Eliminación de la Violencia contra las Mujeres'. Participating establishments are displaying signs in their shop windows from 23 to 25 November. The signs include information and resources for attending victims of gender-based violence. This municipal initiative aims to raise awareness and show collective rejection of violence against women ahead of the 25N International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women.
