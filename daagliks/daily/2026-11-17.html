Torrent approves first phase of La Venteta sports and leisure complex

The city council has approved the first phase of a new outdoor sports and leisure complex in La Venteta, located in Mas del Jutge. The project involves an investment of over €283,000 and includes the construction of a multi-sport court and the urbanization of approximately 1,400 square meters. The broader plan also envisions a children's play area, a 7-a-side football pitch, a padel court, changing rooms, and a social center in later phases.

Torrent to exhibit 1248 Carta de Poblament in Jaume I exhibition

Torrent will display the Carta de Poblament of 1248 at the Casa de la Cultura from 23 to 26 November as part of the exhibition “Jaume I i els nostres Arxius.” The display features historical documents, parchments, facsimiles, and coins marking the 750th anniversary of King Jaume I's death. Three official chroniclers will lead historical outreach sessions dedicated to the final days of the conqueror king and the origins of Torrent.

Guardia Civil dismantles fuel theft network in Torrent and Torrevieja

The Guardia Civil has dismantled a criminal organization involved in the illegal sale of stolen fuel in Torrent and Torrevieja. A total of 58 people were detained in the operation. The group was selling the stolen gasoline at a price of one euro per liter. The investigation highlights the scale of the illicit fuel trade affecting local gas stations in the region.
